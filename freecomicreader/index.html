 MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Just one more page.

  • Add your own comics. Choose CBZ, ZIP or PDF files, or drop them on your shelf. Each file can be up to 250 MB.
  • Read your way. Tap the sides, swipe, or use arrow keys. Tap the middle to hide controls. RTL reverses page direction for manga.
  • Get closer. Choose Zoom (or press Z), then scroll or drag the page. Your browser’s pinch zoom also works.
  • Keep your place. Reading progress saves on this device. Removing an issue here leaves its Drive copy intact.
  • Take it offline. Once “Ready for offline reading” appears, installed pages and downloaded comics work without internet. On iPhone or iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Browser storage can be cleared or evicted. Keep your original files or save a copy to Drive. Private browsing may keep files only for the session.
