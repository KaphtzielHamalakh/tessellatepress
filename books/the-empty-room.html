A novel · Eli Mercer

The Empty Room

He wrote to understand. He listened to be understood. And slowly, the room around him became empty.

Chapters arrive in the mail from a novel published in 1979 that no library holds. The more Eli reads, the more the book knows — his apartment, his marriage, the sentence he was about to think. Behind it stands Julian Voss, a cult science fiction writer who spent eleven years failing to finish the one book that mattered, and a widow whose dead husband's album keeps completing itself without him. Three strands, forty-seven years apart, converging on the same hollow center.
